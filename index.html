What is El Niño and how should I read this section?

El Niño / ENSO: a large-scale change in Pacific Ocean temperatures that can alter rainfall and temperature patterns around the world. It is a global signal, not a country forecast.

Niño 3.4: a commonly used measure of sea-surface temperature in the central tropical Pacific. Positive values mean warmer-than-average water; negative values mean cooler-than-average water.

Observed rainfall: CHIRPS tells us what rainfall has actually been like recently. We compare the latest rolling month with the long-term average.
